# İş İngilizcesi — PM ve Yatırımcı Bağlamı

## 1. Register merdiveni

Aynı mesaj üç seviyede:
- **Resmi e-posta**: "I was wondering if you might be able to share the updated roadmap by Friday."
- **Nötr e-posta**: "Could you share the updated roadmap by Friday?"
- **Slack**: "Hey — any chance you can drop the updated roadmap here by Friday?"

Yumuşatıcılar: could, might, just, quick, perhaps, I was wondering, would you mind -ing.
Kültür notu: İngilizce iş dilinde rica ve red, Türkçedekinden daha fazla yumuşatma bekler. "Send me the file" bir Türk için nötrdür, İngilizcede emir gibi okunur → "Could you send me the file?"

## 2. Toplantı dili

- Söz alma: "May I jump in here?" / "Just to add to that..."
- Nazik itiraz: "I see your point, but..." / "I'm not sure I'd go that far." / "Have we considered...?"
- Netleştirme: "Just to clarify..." / "If I understand correctly, you're saying..."
- Park etme: "Let's take this offline." / "Let's park that for now."
- Özet ve aksiyon: "So, to summarize..." / "The action items are..." / "Who's owning this?"

## 3. PM söz varlığı

- Fiiller: ship, iterate, prioritize, de-scope, de-risk, scope out, punt on, sunset, roll out, align on, sign off on, push back on, flag, surface. ("circle back" işe yarar ama aşırı kullanımı klişedir — söyle.)
- Metrik dili: uptick / downtick, plateau, churn, retention, north star metric, move the needle, leading vs lagging indicator.
- Trade-off dili: "It's a trade-off between X and Y." / "The upside is..., the downside is..." / "We're trading speed for quality here."

## 4. E-posta / Slack kalıpları

- Rica: "Could you..." / "Would you mind -ing..." / "When you get a chance, ..."
- Takip: "Just following up on..." / "Gentle reminder about..." ("kindly reminder" ✗)
- Kötü haber: "Unfortunately, ..." / "I'm afraid..." / "Heads-up: ..."
- Son tarih: "by Friday" (deadline) — "until Friday" değil.
- Kapanış çeşitliliği: "Best," (nötr), "Thanks," (rica sonrası), "Cheers," (samimi, UK).

## 5. Yatırımcı / yönetici dili

- Kısa, iddialı, sayı-önce cümle: "Revenue grew 40% QoQ, driven by enterprise upsells."
- Hedging dengesi: Yatırımcıya aşırı hedge güven kaybettirir ("we might possibly see some growth" ✗); teknik tartışmada sıfır hedge kibirli durur. İkisini ayrı çalıştır.
- Soru yönetimi: "That's a fair question. The short answer is... The longer answer is..."
- Somutlaştırma: "significant growth" yerine sayı; "soon" yerine tarih.

## 6. Mülakat modülü

- **STAR yapısı**: Situation → Task → Action → Result; her cevap 60-90 saniye. Sonucu sayıyla bitir.
- Klasik + PM soruları: "Walk me through a product you shipped." / "Tell me about a time you disagreed with engineering." / "How do you prioritize?" / "Why are you leaving your current role?"
- Güçlü fiiller: led, drove, launched, negotiated, aligned, scoped, delivered, owned. Zayıf kalıp: "I was responsible for" → "I owned / I led".
- Lojistik söz varlığı: notice period, salary expectations, relocation package, equity/stock options, "What does the interview process look like?"
- Prova protokolü: Soruyu sor → cevabı al → içerik geri bildirimi (STAR eksik mi, sonuç sayısız mı) ve dil geri bildirimi (SKILL.md §5) AYRI ver → cevabı bir üst seviyede yeniden söylet.

## 7. Türk profesyonellerin tipik hataları (hızlı tarama listesi)

- "We made a meeting yesterday." → held / had a meeting
- "I will control the document." → review / check
- "Can you send me the informations?" → information
- "We are 5 people in the team." → "There are five of us on the team."
- "I am working at X since 2 years." → "I have been working at X for two years."
- "I have a doubt about the design." → "I have a question about the design."
- "Kindly do the needful." → "Could you take care of this?"
- "Please revert back to me." → "Please get back to me."
- "As per my last email..." → pasif-agresif okunur; "As I mentioned earlier..."
- Her cümlede "as soon as possible" → çeşitlendir: "by EOD Friday" (net), "when you get a chance" (yumuşak), "at your earliest convenience" (resmi)
