## A) ONE SHOT — the sub-15 s single-spectacle series

### Sourced findings

1. **March 2025 made it a two-metric world.** Every replay counts as a public
   view (no minimum watch), but monetization + distribution still run on
   engaged views and retention. A perfect 12 s loop inflates the public
   counter (social proof) *and* pushes APV >100% (real signal) — build loops
   as retention tools, not counter tricks.
   — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/26/youtube-is-changing-how-youtube-shorts-views-are-counted),
   [TubeBuddy](https://www.tubebuddy.com/blog/youtube-shorts-view-count-update-what-creators-need-to-know-about-the-new-metrics/)
2. **Retention percentage beats absolute length** — an 89%-watched 45 s Short
   beats a 53%-watched 15 s one. ⚠︎ For single-event + loop, 10–14 s is the
   sweet spot (under 8 s the event can't settle; over 15 s it stretches).
   Channel's own data (12–14 s, APV ~92%) is the strongest signal we hold.
   — [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/youtube-shorts-length),
   [Virvid](https://virvid.ai/blog/best-shorts-length-retention-2026)
3. **Loop seam must match in motion, not as a freeze.** Perfect loops reach
   APV 150–200%; the viewer re-watches 2–3 times trying to catch the seam.
   ⚠︎ Cleanest ONE SHOT seam: the event is already mid-flight at frame 0, and
   the final frame returns to the same composition.
   — [Joyspace](https://joyspace.ai/looping-hack-trick-algorithm-double-views),
   [Percify](https://percify.io/blog/how-to-make-seamless-loop-videos-a-2026-step-by-step-guide)
4. **Mid-action frame 0 is the 2026 hook standard**; static establishing
   shots read as low-energy and get swiped. Target Viewed-vs-Swiped ≥ 70%.
   — [Virvid](https://virvid.ai/blog/first-3-seconds-hook-faceless-shorts-2026)
5. **Sound is a measured retention lever**: peer-reviewed ASMR work — 50–500 Hz
   raises frontal delta +45% and HF HRV ×10 (relaxation); 500 Hz–10 kHz raises
   beta ×1.2 (the "oddly satisfying" arousal). Impact = bass body, debris tail
   = mid-high texture; both are required. Impact-sound sync IS the ASMR effect.
   — [MDPI Applied Sciences](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/14/22/10223)
6. **Destruction niche**: Teardown-voxel and old low-effort Blender wrecking
   content is saturated; no dominant photoreal rigid-body sub-15 s loop series
   was found (⚠︎ weak negative evidence — treat as a gap signal, not proof).
7. **Posting time**: weekdays 11–14 and 18–21 viewer-local; 17:00 TRT covers
   EU evening + US-East morning; 21:00 TRT worth an A/B. Real answer arrives
   with ~30 videos of Studio data.
   — [HopperHQ](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/best-time-to-post-youtube-shorts/)

### ONE SHOT parameter defaults

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Length | 11–14 s, never >15 |
| Event start | mid-flight at frame 0, impact at t≈0.3–0.8 s |
| Structure | impact → cascade → settle + micro-payoff (last piece standing) → loop frame |
| Sound | 50–500 Hz body at impact, energy-gated bright tail, ~300 ms near-silence before impact ⚠︎; video-time stamps; NO music layer |
| Title | 2–4 words ("One Swing.") — series number as corner badge, not in title |
| Pinned comment | a question that forces a re-watch ("Which floor died first?") |
| Metrics | APV >90% (>100% with loop), VvS ≥70%, first 24–36 h decisive |

## B) IN ORDER — "hit 1→12 in sequence or the song breaks"

### Verdict: GO as the third series, after ESCAPE/MULTIPLY melody episodes
prove out — passes the five-part format test 5/5, but effort M vs their S.

### Sourced findings

- The mechanic exists **split across tools, never combined**: ViralBalls
  "Sequence" mode (numbered segments, ordered hits, wall-break) and
  "Prediction/Target" mode (highlighted targets, near-miss tension) are
  music-less; BallEngine's MIDI note-queue has no targets. Numbered targets +
  melody + wrong-note tension exists nowhere findable. Gap is real.
  — [ViralBalls](https://viralballs.com/en/blog/10-satisfying-ball-physics-video-ideas-that-go-viral),
  [BallEngine](https://ballengine.app/music-ball-simulator)
- **Near-miss psychology** (slot literature): near-misses fire the same
  dopamine paths as wins; digital slots deliberately overrepresent them at
  25–35% vs ~15% natural — a few wrong hits is a proven retention mechanic.
  — [Casino Center](https://www.casinocenter.com/slot-machine-psychology-how-the-near-miss-effect-drives-player-behavior-in-online-gaming/)
- **Deliberate imperfection works**: There I Ruined It, TikTok wrong-note
  piano memes. But the 2026 algorithm punishes videos ending in failure —
  wrong notes are mid-video tension only; the finale always succeeds.
  — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/There_I_Ruined_It),
  [Stanford on rage-bait](https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2025/12/rage-bait-explained-oxford-word-year)
- Industry's legitimate trick: **level design guarantees the outcome, physics
  stays real** (BallEngine computes placements so bounces match the tempo).

### Design parameters

12 pins; per-pin 3.5→1.5 s accelerating; total ~35 s; first hit ≤3 s; wrong-hit
budget 2–3 (never in the last 2 pins); wrong hit = sour note (tritone) + red
flash + counter shake, NO progress reset; armed pin pulses, others dim; finale
= pin 12 sustains last note + chord, loop to opening frame.

### Sim sketch (no scripting, every hit real)

1. 9:16 invisible-container arena, restitution ~1.0 ball, 12 tinted pins,
   pre-created debris parked disabled.
2. Placement = serpentine descent with funnel walls (gauntlet channel rules).
3. **Numbering comes from nature**: run the seed once without breaking,
   record the natural first-contact order, paint numbers in that order; run
   again with break-on-hit. Post-break trajectory divergence produces honest
   sour notes.
4. "Armed" is purely a scoring/audio layer over the contact log; stamp events
   in video time.
5. Selection by sweep (`order_report.py`): 12/12 in order, 28–38 s, 1–3 wrong
   hits, last hit in the final 4 s, no inter-pin gap >5 s; exit 3 otherwise.
