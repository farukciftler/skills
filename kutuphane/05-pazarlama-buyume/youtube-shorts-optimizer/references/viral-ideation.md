# Viral Topics, Insights & Idea Bank

## Non-negotiable: research live, every time

Trends have a lifespan of days to weeks. Anything you "remember" is dead by definition. Before proposing a single idea, run the research pass below with web search. If search is unavailable, say so and clearly label every idea as evergreen-only (no trend claims).

### Research pass (adapt queries to the niche and the channel's language/market)

1. `[niche] YouTube Shorts trending [current month year]` — what's being pushed right now.
2. `[niche] viral TikTok trend this week` — TikTok leads YouTube Shorts by days-to-weeks; early TikTok trends are pre-viral Shorts material.
3. `[niche] news this week` — news hooks and cultural moments give automatic "why now".
4. `trending sounds YouTube Shorts [month year]` — sound/format trends worth riding.
5. If a channel/competitors are known: fetch or search their recent outliers — `site-level: [competitor] shorts` — to find proven formats in the exact niche.
6. For seasonal timing: what is 2–4 weeks out (holidays, releases, events in the target market)? Publishing *before* peak search interest is how small channels win seasonal terms.

Synthesize into 3–5 **current insights** first (one line each, each tied to a source/date), then generate ideas from those insights. Ideas without an insight behind them are guesses.

## Trend lifecycle timing

- **Emerging** (seen on TikTok/small accounts, not yet saturated on Shorts): best risk/reward — recommend fast, rough execution over polish.
- **Peaking** (everywhere on Shorts): only enter with a twist — a niche mashup ("the trend, but in [user's niche]") or a contrarian take. A straight copy at peak gets buried by bigger channels.
- **Decaying** (parodies, "X trend is dead" videos exist): do not recommend, except as the parody itself.

State the lifecycle stage for every trend-based idea.

## Idea bank output format

ALWAYS deliver ideas in this structure, ranked by score, 6–10 ideas:

```
### [N]. [Working title — written as the actual Short title]
- Hook (first line, verbatim): "..."
- Format: [talking head / POV / tutorial / listicle-cut / storytime / green-screen react / loop-gag / before-after ...]
- Why now: [the specific insight/trend/news it rides + lifecycle stage, with date]
- Beats: [3–5 quick beats: 0–2s hook → middle → payoff/loop]
- Effort: S / M / L
- Virality score: X/10 — [one-line reason]
```

Include at least: one emerging-trend ride, one evergreen search-intent idea (compounds for months), one outlier-iteration (if channel data exists), and one contrarian/untapped angle. Diversity of bets beats ten flavors of the same idea.

## Virality scoring rubric (be honest, use the full scale)

Score = average of five dimensions, 1–10 each:
1. **Hook strength** — can the first second stop a swipe from a stranger?
2. **Emotional charge** — curiosity, awe, outrage, humor, "that's so me". Neutral = low.
3. **Shareability** — is there a person the viewer would send this to? Identity content ("tag a friend who…") and useful content share best.
4. **Timing** — emerging trend or fresh news = high; peaked = medium; stale = low.
5. **Fit & repeatability** — can THIS creator execute it credibly, and can it become a series? One-off stunts score lower here even if flashy.

7+ = drop other plans and make it this week. 5–6 = solid pipeline material. <5 = don't present it.

## Hook line formulas (for the verbatim hook field)

Cold-open mid-action beats any greeting. Options: bold claim ("Nobody is telling you this about X"), stakes ("I have 24 hours to fix this"), open question ("Why does every X do this?"), visual promise ("Watch what happens at second 12"), identity call-out ("If you edit videos, stop scrolling"), in-media-res dialogue. The hook must be honest — the payoff has to exist, or retention + satisfaction surveys kill the video and the channel's audience model with it.

## Insight framing

When the user asks for "insights", deliver them as decisions, not observations. Not "educational content is growing in your niche" but "your niche's top 3 growing channels all moved to 20–30s single-tip formats in the last 60 days (sources) — your 60s multi-tip format is fighting the current; split each video into 3."
