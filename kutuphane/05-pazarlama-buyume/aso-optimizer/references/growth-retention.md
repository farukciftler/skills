# Growth, Retention & Algorithm Signals

## In-App Events / Promotional Content
- **Apple In-App Events**: Cards appear in search, Today tab, and app product pages.
- **Google Promotional Content**: Featured in Games/Apps tabs, Search, and Store listings.
- Key event types: Competitions, Live Stream, Special Offer, Major Update, New Season.

## Post-Install Retention as a Ranking Factor
- 2026 store algorithms penalize apps with high 1-day/7-day uninstall rates ("keyword stuffing without product delivery").
- High retention, long session lengths, and positive review sentiment elevate organic ranking.

## Ratings & Review Sentiment Analysis
- Prompt ratings in-app after a positive milestone (e.g., completing a task, winning a level).
- AI algorithms parse review text for keywords; responding to reviews with relevant context boosts trust signals.
