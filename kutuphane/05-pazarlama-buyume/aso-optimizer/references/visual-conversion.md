# Visual Asset & Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

## Screenshot OCR Indexing (iOS 2026)
- Apple uses OCR technology to read and index text rendered inside app screenshots.
- Place strategic secondary keywords in screenshot headline banners.
- Ensure high contrast between text and background for maximum OCR extraction.

## Screenshot Design Best Practices
- **Portrait vs Landscape**: Use portrait unless the app/game is strictly landscape.
- **First 3 Screenshots**: Account for 80%+ of user conversion decisions. Focus on top core benefits.
- **Social Proof & Badges**: Display awards, rating badges, or user metrics on later screenshots.
- **Device Frames**: Use clean, modern device borders that do not obstruct app UI elements.

## Custom Product Pages (CPPs) & PPO Strategy
- **iOS CPPs**: Up to 35 custom product pages per app. Unique URLs for targeted ad campaigns.
- **Google Custom Listings**: Up to 50 custom store listings by user location/country or campaign.
- **Product Page Optimization (PPO)**: Test icon variants, screenshots, and promo videos against control group.
