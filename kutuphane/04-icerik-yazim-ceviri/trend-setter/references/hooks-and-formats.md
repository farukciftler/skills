# Hooks and Formats

The hook earns the next three seconds; the structure earns the rest. Both must pass the humanize gate — a hook built from banned phrases ("Here's the thing:", "Unpopular opinion:") is worse than a plain one, because clichés are pattern-matched and skipped.

## 1. Hook psychology

Scrolling brains run on autopilot, pattern-matching for anything that breaks the expected flow. Four levers create the break:

- **Pattern interrupt** — unexpected visual, claim, or tone. In 2026 this cuts both ways: fast cuts still work, but deliberate *calm* (a pause, a quiet frame, a slow sentence) now interrupts feeds full of noise.
- **Curiosity gap** — open a loop the viewer must close. The content MUST close it; unpaid curiosity debt destroys trust and follow-through metrics.
- **Specificity** — "How I cut our AWS bill" is wallpaper; "How one idle NAT Gateway cost us $840/month for 11 months" stops thumbs.
- **Stakes** — why it matters to *this* viewer, now. Name the audience or the loss.

## 2. Hook library

Each type with an English and a Turkish example. Adapt substance, never copy shells verbatim across posts — repeated shells become the account's own tell.

1. **Contrarian / negative** — attack a common practice.
   - "Stop A/B testing your hooks. You don't have the traffic for it."
   - "Cloud maliyet raporu ayda bir geliyorsa, o rapor süs."
2. **Specific outcome** — number + timeframe + method tease.
   - "0 to 4,200 newsletter subs in 90 days. One channel did 80% of it."
   - "Bir cron job'la aylık 12 saat manuel işi sıfırladık. Kod 40 satır."
3. **Open loop** — start a story mid-tension.
   - "The client fired us on a Tuesday. Best thing that happened to the product."
   - "Demo'dan 10 dakika önce staging çöktü. Sunumu yine de yaptık."
4. **Direct callout** — name the audience in line one.
   - "If you run FinOps for a company under 200 people, skip the enterprise tooling."
   - "Yeni PM'ler: ilk 90 gününüzde roadmap yazmayın."
5. **Mistake / confession** — earned credibility through cost.
   - "I shipped a pricing page that lost us money for six weeks. Here's the bug."
   - "3.700 dolarlık hatamı anlatayım da siz yapmayın."
6. **Warning** — protective urgency.
   - "Before you sign that SaaS annual plan, check one clause."
   - "Sponsorlu içerik anlaşması imzalamadan önce şu maddeye bakın."
7. **Sharp question** — only if the answer is genuinely non-obvious. Generic engagement questions ("What do you think?") are dead weight.
   - "Why do the best-retaining Reels get *worse* CPMs?"
   - "Neden en çok kaydedilen postlar en az beğeni alıyor?"
8. **Data shock** — one stat, no framing.
   - "70% completion. That's the viral threshold on TikTok now. It was 50% in 2024."
9. **POV / scene** — drop into a concrete moment (video-native).
   - "POV: it's 7pm Friday, the POS just died, and the kitchen is understaffed."
10. **Listicle promise** — works when the number is odd/specific and the list is real.
    - "7 Jira automations that survived contact with an actual dev team."

**Retired hooks (2026 clichés — do not use):** "Unpopular opinion:", "Hot take:", "Nobody talks about...", "Read that again.", "Let that sink in.", "I said what I said.", "Hadi başlayalım", "Kimse bunu konuşmuyor ama".

## 3. Hook rules

- Write 5–8 hook candidates, pick the best, offer the runner-up 2 as alternates in delivery.
- The hook must be true. Redeemable curiosity only — the body pays off exactly what the hook promised.
- Video: hook lands in three channels at once (visual + spoken + on-screen text) within 1.5 seconds.
- Text: the hook works standalone above the fold (LinkedIn ~200 chars, X first line, IG caption first line).
- Never open with context. Open at the point of highest tension, backfill context in line two.

## 4. Format blueprints

### Story-arc post (LinkedIn, long X post)
Hook (tension) → context in one breath → the turn (what changed / what was learned) → the takeaway as a claim, not a summary → close on a question or punchline. Paragraphs of 1–2 sentences, white space between all of them. 900–1.800 characters. One story, one lesson — two lessons means two posts.

### Listicle (thread, carousel, or long post)
Promise with a specific count → items, each carrying a *why* and a concrete *how* (an item without a number, tool name, or example gets cut) → close with the sharpest item's implication or a question. Never close with a recap of the list; the reader just read it.

### Carousel narrative (design specs in `design-system.md`)
- Slide 1: hook — biggest type, one focal idea, zero clutter
- Slide 2: re-hook — the stake, the cost, or the "why now"
- Slides 3–8: one idea per slide, consistent layout, a progress cue (numbers or a bar)
- Last slide: discussion question or one clear CTA; handle + save prompt
Copy per slide: max ~25 words. The caption carries the nuance, slides carry the skeleton.

### Myth vs. reality / Before vs. after
Pairs format; strongest when the "myth" is something the audience actually believes (test: would a real practitioner defend it?). Two-column visuals or alternating beats in video.

### Teardown / build-in-public
Walk through a real artifact — a landing page, a bill, a dashboard, a metric graph. Screenshots beat stock imagery on every platform. Series-friendly: "Teardown #14" formats train return viewers, and serialized content is one of the strongest 2026 patterns.

### Edutainment bit
Teach one thing inside an entertainment shell (skit, meme format, trend sound). The trend supplies the shell; the niche supplies the substance (see `trend-research.md`). If the joke survives but the lesson doesn't, it failed.

## 5. Short-video script template

Deliver video scripts in this table shape:

| Time | Spoken | On-screen text | Shot / b-roll |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–1.5s | verbal hook | text hook (≤6 words) | payoff frame or pattern interrupt |
| 1.5–5s | re-hook, stakes | key number | cut or push-in |
| 5–25s | payload beats (cut every 1.5–3s) | captions on | screen recs / hands / product |
| close | loop line or question | CTA (≤4 words) | return to opening frame if looping |

Notes to include with every script: target length with a reason, the retention risk point (where people will swipe and what defends it), and the loop mechanic if any.

## 6. CTA bank

Corporate CTAs ("Hemen tıklayın!", "Don't miss out!") are banned. Use natives:

- **Save trigger:** "Save this for your next invoice review." / "Fatura günü lazım olur, kaydet."
- **Share trigger:** "Send this to the friend who still exports to Excel manually." / "Bunu hâlâ her şeyi elle yapan arkadaşına at."
- **Comment farm (honest):** ask the question you actually want answered. "What's the dumbest cloud cost you've found?" / "Sizin bulduğunuz en saçma bulut masrafı neydi?"
- **Soft product CTA:** one line, after value, never in the hook. "We built a tool for exactly this — link in comments."
- **Follow CTA:** earn it with a series promise. "Ep. 3 of the FinOps teardowns — 12 more coming."

One CTA per piece. A post asking for a like, a save, a share, AND a follow gets none of them.
