# Trend Research — Live, Never From Memory

Trend knowledge expires in days to weeks. Any claim about "what's working right now", a trending sound, a meme format, or a current algorithm change must come from a web search run *today*, dated with the current month and year. Training-data trends are, by definition, dead trends.

## Search recipes

Run 2–4 of these, adapted to the task (always append the current month + year):

- `[platform] trends [month year]` — format-level shifts
- `[niche] [platform] trend this week` — niche-specific waves
- `trending audio TikTok [month year]` / `trending Reels audio [month year]` — sound trends (shortest shelf life: 1–2 weeks)
- `[platform] algorithm update [month year]` — before making any mechanical claim
- For Türkiye: `Türkiye TikTok trend [ay yıl]`, `Türkiye X gündem [konu]`, plus checking whether a global trend has a local variant already (Turkish audiences adopt global formats with a 1–3 week lag and a local twist — the lag is the opportunity)

Prefer primary and fresh sources: platform creator blogs and newsrooms, the last 30 days of Buffer/Later/Hootsuite/Sprout posts, creator case studies with numbers. Treat listicles older than ~6 weeks as history, not intel.

## The trend-fit filter

Before adapting any trend, score it quickly against four questions; two "no"s means skip it:

1. **Relevance** — can the niche's substance live inside this format naturally, or is it forced? Forced trend-jumping reads desperate and damages the account's semantic topic-consistency (which 2026 feeds measure).
2. **Freshness window** — sounds die in 1–2 weeks, visual/format trends live 1–3 months, structural trends (serialization, edutainment, long-form comeback) run for years. Match effort to remaining lifespan; never recommend a trend at its saturation peak.
3. **Brand safety** — origin and adjacent usage of the trend checked? Some sounds/memes carry baggage invisible at first glance.
4. **Originality headroom** — platforms suppress verbatim trend copies (Instagram scores originality explicitly). There must be a real adaptation, not a re-enactment.

## Adaptation formula

**Trend shell + niche substance + brand voice.** The trend supplies the recognizable container (sound, cut pattern, caption format, joke structure); the niche supplies the actual information or story; the voice keeps it on-brand. If removing the trend would leave nothing valuable, there's nothing there — skip.

## Portfolio balance

Recommend content mixes around 70/20/10: 70% evergreen value in the account's core topics, 20% trend-adapted, 10% experimental formats. Trend content buys reach; evergreen content converts it into followers and trust. An account that is *only* trends has no semantic identity for the feed to match against.

## Trend brief format

When the deliverable is trend research itself, cap it at 5 trends, each in four lines:
1. **What it is** — one sentence, with a source link
2. **Why it fits** — the niche connection, honestly assessed
3. **The adaptation** — a concrete, shootable/writable idea (not "you could make a video about...")
4. **Window** — how many days/weeks of life remain and the recommended ship-by date
